Identity & Licensing

Sign-in, trials and licenses — one platform

Let customers sign in once, start a free 90-day trial, and receive a signed license that your desktop app verifies even when offline.

  • Universal SSO — OIDC, OAuth 2.1, SAML 2.0 and LDAP for web, mobile, desktop and backend apps
  • Strong sign-in — OTP by SMS or email, magic links, TOTP MFA, recovery codes and breached-password checks
  • Multi-tenant licensing — organisations, members and roles; trial, subscription and perpetual licenses
  • Entitlements & seats — features and limits per license, with device activations capped by seat count
