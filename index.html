FAQ

Common questions

Have something else on your mind? We reply to all emails within one business day.

Do you work with early-stage startups?
+
Absolutely. Some of our best long-term relationships started with a founder and a Figma file. We offer a flexible Starter package and can scope projects to match pre-seed and seed-stage budgets.
How long does a typical project take?
+
Starter projects usually wrap in 3–4 weeks. Full Growth engagements run 8–14 weeks depending on complexity. We'll give you a detailed timeline in our project scope document before work begins.
What tools do you design in?
+
All design work is done in Figma. We deliver organized, named, and documented files with auto-layout components and design tokens. Prototypes are also built in Figma unless you need something more elaborate.
Do you do development as well as design?
+
Yes. Our Growth package includes full frontend development in React/Next.js. We can also work with your existing engineering team by providing pixel-perfect specs and component documentation.
What does your onboarding look like?
+
We start every engagement with a 60-minute discovery call. From there we produce a project brief, timeline, and payment schedule within 48 hours. Most projects kick off within one week of signing.
